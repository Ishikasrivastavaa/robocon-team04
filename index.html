01 / THE MISSION

Not just fast.
Predictably in control.

The challenge is to carry a payload of up to 5 kg through a marked factory-like course, tracking straight sections and changing curves without losing the intended path.

As first-year engineering students, our focus is to turn the problem statement into a real, testable system — one subsystem at a time. We document what works, what still needs debugging, and what must be validated before making performance claims.

ROLE / EMBEDDED CONTROL

ESP32-S3

Primary embedded controller for the current architecture. Final pin mapping and processing responsibilities must be checked against the actual integration plan.

NEXT CHECKConfirm pin map, supply rails and signal levels.
CHECK 01

LOGIC-LEVEL SAFETY

ESP32-S3 GPIO is 3.3 V logic. Verify sensor output levels and use appropriate level-shifting or interface circuitry before connecting any 5 V signal.

CHECK 02

MOTOR CURRENT

Driver selection must account for motor voltage, expected load, continuous current and startup/stall current—not just a nominal RPM label.

CHECK 03

POWER STABILITY

Servo and motor transients can disturb controller power. Validate power distribution, grounding and regulator capacity under real load.
